About

I don't just edit — I help brands scale with creative that converts.

I'm a video editor specializing in performance creative for ecom brands. My work lives at the intersection of aesthetics and conversion — every cut, every hook, every second of pacing is built to do a job.

I understand what makes people stop scrolling, keep watching, and click buy. From high-production paid ads to raw UGC-style content, I adapt to what the audience needs and what the funnel demands — and I move fast while doing it.

Why I'm the Right Fit

01
I Think in Hooks, Not Just Cuts
Most editors execute briefs. I think about why the video needs to exist — what stops the scroll, what triggers curiosity, what earns the next second of attention. The strategy starts before the timeline opens.
02
I Understand Ecom Creative Strategy
I'm constantly studying why ads work — what hooks hold attention, why some creatives die in three days while others keep spending. Every edit is a chance to test a theory and get better at the craft.
03
Fast Output, No Excuses
Ecom moves fast. Creative testing never stops. I deliver first drafts in 24–48h, handle revisions same day, and maintain quality across multiple iterations without slowing down your pipeline.
04
Versatile Across Formats & Styles
High-production paid ads. Raw UGC. Cinematic brand films. AI-assisted creatives. I adapt to your brand's voice and platform — Meta, TikTok, YouTube — and nail the native feel every time.

What I Do

Paid Ad Editing
Meta, TikTok, YouTube. Hook-first editing built for paid performance. Every second earns its place or gets cut.
UGC Editing
Raw footage → polished, scroll-stopping ad. Captions, pacing, sound design — the full package, native feel intact.
AI-Assisted Creative
Higgsfield, Midjourney, Runway — combining AI visuals with real footage for next-level creatives that don't look AI.
Long Form Editing
YouTube, brand films, documentary-style content. Retention-optimized pacing, clean structure, strong narrative arc.
Motion Graphics
Titles, captions, transitions, lower thirds. Clean motion that enhances without distracting from the message.
Creative Iteration
I don't make one version and call it done. I build creative systems — hook variants, format tests, b-roll swaps — built for scaling.
